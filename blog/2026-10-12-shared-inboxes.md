Support teams kept asking for one thing: a way to work the same inbox without stepping on each other. Shared inboxes ship today on every plan.

<!-- truncate -->

![Shared inboxes](/img/shared-inboxes.svg)

## What you can do

- **Assign** a thread to yourself or a teammate with one click.
- **See who's typing.** Beacon shows when someone else is drafting a reply.
- **Hand off** a thread with an internal note that the customer never sees.

## Setting it up

Go to **Settings → Inboxes**, add the address you want to share, and invite your team. Beacon gives you a forwarding address to point your existing mailbox at.
