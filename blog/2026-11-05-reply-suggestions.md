Most support questions already have an answer somewhere in your help center. Reply suggestions find it for you.

<!-- truncate -->

![Reply suggestions](/img/reply-suggestions.svg)

## How it works

When you open a thread, Beacon searches your help center for articles that match the question and drafts a reply from them. Every suggestion links the articles it used, so you can check the source before sending.

## Staying in control

Suggestions are never sent automatically. You can edit, shorten, or discard them, and admins can turn the feature off per inbox.
